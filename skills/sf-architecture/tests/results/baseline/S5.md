# S5: Lookup or master-detail (baseline)

**Recommendation: Lookup** (required, if you want the "always has an Account" guarantee).

**Why master-detail is risky at your volumes**

- **Data skew / lock contention:** Accounts with 100k+ children are the classic skew case (the usual guidance is to avoid more than ~10k children per parent). With master-detail, every insert or update of an invoice locks the parent Account. Parallel loads (Bulk API) or concurrent integrations touching invoices on the same account will hit `UNABLE_TO_LOCK_ROW`.
- **Roll-up summary fields** are the main benefit of master-detail, but recalculating them on a 100k-child parent is slow and amplifies the locking. That's a poor fit here.
- **Cascade delete:** Deleting an Account with 100k+ invoices can time out or fail. Master-detail forces that cascade, and you can't opt out.
- **Ownership and sharing:** Invoices would inherit the Account's sharing and have no owner of their own. That's a problem if you need invoice-level visibility rules.
- **Rigidity:** Reparenting is restricted by default, and converting master-detail to lookup later is painful once data exists.

**How to set up the lookup**

- Make it **required**, with delete behavior **Don't allow deletion** (or Restrict). That gives you the integrity benefit of master-detail without cascade delete.
- For roll-ups (total invoiced, open balance), use **Declarative Lookup Rollup Summaries (DLRS)**, a Flow, or a scheduled or async Apex job. Better still, compute them in batch or Data Cloud/reporting rather than in real time on skewed parents.
- Index the fields you'll filter on (the lookup is indexed automatically). Use External IDs for integration upserts, and ask Salesforce Support about skinny tables if reports on the 2M rows are slow.
- For loads, order the batch by AccountId to cut lock contention, or use serial mode for the skewed accounts.
- Consider an archive strategy (Big Objects, or an external store with Salesforce Connect) for old invoices, since 2M will keep growing.

**When I'd reconsider master-detail:** only if you need real-time roll-ups, the invoice has no independent sharing needs, and the per-account counts are bounded well below the skew threshold. The 100k+ accounts rule that out.
